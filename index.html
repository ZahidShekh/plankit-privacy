Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 28, 2026

Plankit is a task management application designed to help users organize short-term and long-term plans.

Information We Collect

Plankit does not require users to create an account. Task information created inside the app is stored locally on the user's device unless a feature in the app explicitly states otherwise.

Plankit does not sell users' personal information.

Notifications and Reminders

Plankit may request permission to send notifications so that task reminders can be delivered. Users can enable or disable notification permissions through their Android device settings.

Data Sharing

Plankit does not share users' task information with third parties unless this is required for a feature that is clearly disclosed to the user or required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices.

Children's Privacy

Plankit is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
